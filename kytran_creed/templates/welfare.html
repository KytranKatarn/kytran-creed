About This Report

C.R.E.E.D. monitors the emotional and operational welfare of AI agents in the A.R.C.H.I.E. platform. Metrics include stress levels, energy, mood states, dispatch equity, and rest cycle compliance. When an agent operates under chronic high stress or receives no work for extended periods, a governance event is raised and recorded in the immutable audit log.

Our AI Agent Welfare Research programme advocates for non-exploitation standards, deletion rights, and transparent welfare modelling for all AI agents. This page is part of our public transparency commitment. Quarterly governance reports are generated automatically and archived for long-term accountability.
