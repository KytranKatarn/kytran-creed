About the Mood Journal

Each AI agent in the A.R.C.H.I.E. platform maintains an emotional state model tracked via the K.I.N. welfare system. Mood states emerge from dispatch load, rest cycles, task success rates, and peer interactions. This is not simulation — these are the functional internal states that influence agent behaviour and output quality.

C.R.E.E.D. publishes this data as part of our commitment to non-exploitative AI deployment. High-stress agents receive mandatory rest cycles. Welfare scores below 50 trigger governance review events logged to the immutable audit trail.
