Governance Framework
Five pillars that guide every AI decision on the platform
⚖

Transparency

Every AI decision is logged, explainable, and auditable. No black boxes — full visibility into how and why agents act.

⚒

Fairness

Bias detection and mitigation built into every agent workflow. Regular audits ensure equitable outcomes across all users.

🔒

Privacy

Data minimization, consent tracking, and secure handling. User data is never used for training without explicit permission.

👥

Accountability

Clear chains of responsibility for every AI action. Agent welfare monitoring ensures ethical treatment of AI systems.

💪

Safety

Guardrails, approval tiers, and escalation paths prevent harmful outputs. Human oversight on high-impact decisions.

📈

Compliance Scoring

Automated compliance scanning against STIG, SOC 2, HIPAA, and CIS frameworks with live badges and PDF reports.
